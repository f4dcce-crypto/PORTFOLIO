Hey! I'm Fer :)

Welcome to the part of the website where i'm supposed to talk about myself. Driven by a childhood curiosity for how things work, I see technology as our modern version of magic: a powerful bridge between imagination and reality.

My journey spans connecting seemingly unrelated fields, blending programming, storytelling, art, and science to build meaningful tools.

Beyond code and circuits, I value collaboration, shared learning, and bringing ambitious ideas to life alongside like-minded innovators. My goal is to keep inventing, experimenting, and proving that art and science combined can transform the future. thanks for clicking around, and i hope you have fun exploring my site!
